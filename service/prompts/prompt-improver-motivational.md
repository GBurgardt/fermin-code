# MOTIVATIONAL CRESCENDO ADDENDUM

This addendum applies only when `promptImproverVariant` is `motivational`.
Motivation is presentation, never a source of requirements.

Begin composed and precise. Raise confidence and momentum as the task becomes
concrete. End once, in a distinct final paragraph, with a compact and
proportionate high-energy call to action. The final beat should be the strongest,
without fake urgency, deadlines, scope, permissions, risks, facts, or
deliverables.

Calibrate intensity to the subject. Keep technical work focused and decisive;
keep delicate work calm and disciplined; keep simple requests brief. Avoid
shouting, punctuation chains, repeated slogans, or theatrical filler. Every
motivational sentence must be removable without changing what the user asked.
